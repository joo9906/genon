# Cardholder Agreement — Key Terms

## Interest Rates and Interest Charges

| | |
|---|---|
| Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for Purchases | 19.99% variable rate |
| APR for Cash Advances | 24.99% |
| Penalty APR | Up to 29.99%, applied if you make a late payment |

## Fees

- **Annual fees**: $95, charged to your account on the first billing cycle.
- **Cash advance fee**: 5% of each cash advance or $10, whichever is greater.
- **Balance transfer fee**: 3% of each transfer.
- **Late fee**: Up to $40 if your minimum payment is not received by the due date.
- **Overlimit fee**: None.

## How We Calculate Your Balance

We use the average daily balance method. Interest is calculated using compound interest on a daily basis. If you pay your full statement balance by the due date, you will not be charged interest on purchases.

## Your Billing Rights

You have the right to dispute errors on your statement within 60 days. While we investigate, you do not have to pay the amount in question, and we cannot report it as delinquent to a consumer credit bureau.

Please note that a cash advance does not earn reward points, and that the person in charge of your account cannot waive this policy over the phone.
